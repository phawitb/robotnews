# Sources and editorial selection

Discover from multiple independent sources; this is a starting list, not a fixed whitelist:

- The Robot Report: `https://www.therobotreport.com/`
- RoboticsTomorrow: `https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/`
- IEEE Spectrum: `https://spectrum.ieee.org/` (robotics coverage)
- Robot-New: `https://robot-new.com/news/` (discovery; follow originals)
- TechCrunch: `https://techcrunch.com/` (robotics coverage)
- MIT News: `https://news.mit.edu/` (robotics research)
- Official newsrooms of the companies/universities involved, used to verify claims.

Find currently working feeds or category pages from each site's actual links; do not invent RSS paths. Syndicated releases across publishers count as one event and not independent evidence. Prefer a source with a complete article and direct media; trace aggregators to original reporting. Clearly attribute company-provided performance claims and distinguish research prototypes from available products.

Maintain candidate notes: actual publication time/timezone and evidence, canonical URLs, company/product/event, proposed storyKey, prior-story comparison, four scoring dimensions, selection/rejection reason, source reliability and full media audit. Use these notes to choose ten, not merely the first ten search results. Avoid ten near-identical launches when stronger variety is available. No source or story is required if it has no fresh qualifying item today.
